# Cycling 21st-Century Ranking

## 📌 Project Overview
This personal project aims to objectively determine the best professional cyclist of the 21st century (2000–present) using a prestige-weighted scoring system in Python. Instead of merely counting raw victories, it applies a tailored points scale to Grands Tours, Monuments, and major races to fairly evaluate riders across different specialties (climbers, sprinters, time-trialists, and classics specialists).

## 💡 Origin & Motivation
While I am deeply invested in competitive sports, I have always had a lifelong passion for professional cycling. Ever since I was a kid, I have followed almost every single race on the calendar. 

Between the ages of 10 and 15, I spent much of my free time in my room rating professional cyclists across various attributes—such as climbing, sprinting, time-trialing, and cobbles—and then simulating the Tour de France and the entire cycling season using metal cycling figurines based on those ratings. 

As I grew older and developed a strong affinity for Python, econometrics, and data analysis, translating this childhood pastime into a coded project felt like a natural step. It combines something I genuinely love with technical skill-building. Ultimately, this project will allow us to uncover fascinating historical trends and statistical insights from modern cycling.
